### Detailed Profiles

#### 1. Lockheed Martin (NYSE: LMT)
- **HQ**: 6801 Rockledge Drive, Bethesda, MD 20817
- **Status**: Q3 2026 sales $20.7B (+11% YoY). Net earnings $1.8B. Record backlog $292B (up 27% QoQ), including $52.5B from PAC-3 MSE multi-year. Raised FY2026 guidance to $80-82B sales. F-35: 10-year framework for ~850 US aircraft at 156/year rate. Negotiations ongoing on Lot 20+. Strong book-to-bill.
- **Major Programs**: F-35 Lightning II, missiles (PAC-3, Javelin JV), space, C4ISR, helicopters (Sikorsky).
- **Locations**: Primary HQ Bethesda; major sites in Fort Worth/Grand Prairie TX, Orlando FL, etc. Expanding manufacturing.
- **Recent**: ARRW hypersonic production start; various Navy/Army awards.

#### 2. RTX Corporation (NYSE: RTX) - formerly Raytheon Technologies
- **HQ**: 1000 Wilson Blvd, Arlington, VA 22209
- **Business Units**:
  - Raytheon: Missiles, radars, defense electronics (HQ Arlington)
  - Pratt & Whitney: Engines (East Hartford, CT)
  - Collins Aerospace: Avionics, systems (Charlotte, NC)
- **Status**: Consistent top defense contractor. Recent awards include SM-6 production, SPY-6 radar mods, F135 engine sustainment (~$1.6B), AN/TPY-2 support. Missile defense and munitions strong amid global demand.
- **Locations**: Corporate HQ Arlington VA; extensive footprint across US and globally.

#### 3. The Boeing Company (NYSE: BA)
- **Global HQ**: 929 Long Bridge Drive, Arlington, VA 22202
- **Defense, Space & Security HQ**: Recently moved back to St. Louis area (from Arlington Crystal City) in Feb 2026 to be closer to production/workforce (~18k employees in region).
- **Status**: Defense obligations ~$28.7B (last 12 mo). Key programs: KC-46 tankers (ongoing mods), F/A-18, Apache, new next-gen fighters (F-47 Air Force, F/A-XX Navy). CEO notes better contract structures (cost-plus for some new programs) to avoid past fixed-price losses. Commercial recovery ongoing.
- **Locations**: Arlington (gov ops/HQ), St. Louis (defense production), Seattle (commercial), Charleston SC, etc.

#### 4. General Dynamics (NYSE: GD)
- **HQ**: 11011 Sunset Hills Road, Reston, VA 20190
- **Segments**: Aerospace (Gulfstream), Marine Systems (Electric Boat - Groton CT; Bath Iron Works), Combat Systems (tanks - Sterling Heights MI), Technologies (GDIT).
- **Status**: Strong in submarines (Virginia-class, Columbia-class). Electric Boat often tops FY2026 provisional obligations. Various engineering/support awards.
- **Locations**: Reston VA HQ; major production in CT, ME, MI, etc.

#### 5. Northrop Grumman (NYSE: NOC)
- **HQ**: Falls Church, VA (typical)
- **Status**: Space systems, B-21 Raider, autonomous/unmanned, radars. Recent Triton, AESA radar awards.
- **Locations**: Northern VA area.

## Broader Trends
- **HQ Locations**: Most major defense primes sit in the Northern Virginia / Greater DC area (Arlington, Reston, Bethesda, Falls Church) for proximity to Pentagon, Congress, and customers. Production and engineering often elsewhere (TX, CT, FL, etc.).
- **Relocations**: Broader corporate HQ trend toward Texas (Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, Houston) from high-cost coastal areas (CA, NY), driven by taxes, regulation, talent, costs. Defense primes largely stay near DC; Boeing partially shifted defense ops to St. Louis.
- **Contract Environment**: High defense spending (DoD/Department of War ~$400B+ in contracts). Focus on munitions, missiles, ships, aircraft sustainment, IT/cloud, border infrastructure. IDIQs and multi-year awards common for visibility.
- **Status Overview**: Backlogs at record levels for primes like Lockheed due to multi-year awards. Ongoing awards for sustainment, production, R&D. Some programs face production rate challenges or negotiations.
